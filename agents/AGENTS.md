## Architecture and authority

Use **Forward**, with isolated role Sessions dispatched by a restricted local orchestration relay. The relay is a trusted deterministic control-plane component, not a trading LLM or an additional strategy author. Its implementation and verified deployment remain release blockers. It alone owns the durable append journal, dispatch registry, account lease, approval state and permission-event responses.

| Role | Specification | Desired capability | Forbidden |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinator | `coordinator.md` | Relay dispatch and summaries | Moomoo Vault/tools, account execution |
| Scout | `scout.md` | Validated market/news reads; evidence or HOLD veto | Orders, account access, new strategy/target invention |
| Guard | `guard.md` | Account/market reads; sole paper-order workflow | Unaudited execution, live trading, discretionary strategy |
| Risk Auditor | `risk-auditor.md` | Separate Session; account reads, plan acceptance or veto | Orders, strategy creation, target editing |
| Advisor | `advisor.md` | Tool-free second opinion on sanitized input | Tools, credentials, approval authority, execution |

**Never let the executor act on the researcher's output without the auditor's approval.** Scout evidence is not an order instruction. An auditor's no-veto/plan acceptance is not order permission. A second LLM opinion is not independent financial evidence.

Native Forward child Threads share Vault access and filesystem; thread-level conversation separation is not a security boundary. Do not place privileged Guard and research roles in a shared native parent environment. Risk Auditor always runs in its own isolated Session. Native Collaboration/Advisor may be used only where credential, tool and filesystem isolation can be demonstrated; otherwise use isolated role dispatch and return sanitized advice. Do not claim native child historical-version pinning: the supplied `sessions/update` documentation constraint says version resolves to latest and a supplied historical version is ignored.

## Order lifecycle

1. Relay verifies the release lock and creates isolated Sessions with minimal allowlists. Acquire a durable per-mode/account single-writer lease with fencing token; concurrent or stale owners fail closed.
2. Read and reconcile current account funds, positions, open orders and fills. Unknown submission state forbids resubmission. Verify the authoritative paper account: test **5322438**, competition **5832812**. Never infer mode from the first account returned.
3. Validate immutable frozen-strategy targets, config provenance, completed-session data, checksum and position snapshot. Scout may veto with HOLD but cannot rewrite weights. Missing, corrupt, future, stale or unknown-borrow inputs mean HOLD/HALT, not guessed defaults.
4. Guard constructs a positions-diff plan against the actual broker holdings, including the P\* long and short sleeve legs, pending orders, residual partial fills, the liquidation reserve and risk reductions. Positions the journal does not attribute to this strategy version are planning input only and never appear in the diff. No order tools are used to plan.
5. Risk Auditor independently reads account state and validates the proposed plan. Its acceptance binds **plan hash, target checksum, account snapshot checksum, config checksum, mode/account, run/session, fencing token and expiry**. Audit every proposed plan. Target changes invalidate approval; any material plan/account change requires replanning and re-audit.
6. In test mode, obtain explicit approval for **every order, including exits, cancels and replacements**, expiring after **five minutes**, for the exact payload. Slack consent cannot waive a hard rule. This release grants no autonomous competition-order approval.
7. All paper-order tools are configured **always_ask**. Guard uses only `paper-trading-gate`. The trusted relay answers the actual permission event only after inspecting the **actual raw tool payload**, verified tool identity/schema, account and mode, approved fingerprint, the full R0–R21 results and the additional checks. It must not trust an LLM's payload summary or a claimed approval flag. Unknown tools/fields or unverifiable interception are denied. No Bash, network, curl, alternate MCP, or trading CLI workaround.
8. Immediately before dispatch, recheck freshness, approval/lease validity, order budget and kill state; atomically consume one-use permission and reserve the order/exit capacity. Journal the intent durably before any side effect. Missing journal durability means deny.
9. Submitted is not filled. Reconcile broker order/fill IDs, partials, remaining quantity and fees. Timeout means unknown, not permission to retry. Replacing an expired order requires known cancellation/terminal state, a new diff/audit when changed, and fresh exact-payload approval. Release the lease only after recoverable state is durable.

The main gate must implement R0–R21 (R0 contract plus R1–R21), together with approval, session lock, kill latch, freshness, journal and idempotency checks, dispatched by `config["strategy"]`: `rules/gate_pstar.py` for P\* and `rules/gate.py` for the retired plan A. Both return the full result with `strategy` and, for P\*, `variant`, and `execution_allowed` additionally requires `execution_enabled`, which is false in every configuration in this release. These docs do not renumber or replace either rule table. Any missing rule or unknown result fails closed. ## Fixed strategy and risk parameters

The deployment default is the frozen portfolio **P\***, defined only in `research/r10_deploy.py`. `live/config.pstar.json` repeats its constants and selects the variant (`pstar15`, round 14), and `live/test_pstar_replay.py` fails if the two ever disagree. ### Deployed: P\*

- Book: **T9**, an ETF trend sleeve over SPY, QQQ, IWM, TLT, IEF, GLD, SLV, USO and UUP, plus **D77**, a 77-name broad short book scored by literature-signed factors with **no estimation** at all (`pstar`: 11 factors; `pstar15` adds cfp, op, cop and sg). The two sleeves are blended by Ledoit-Wolf minimum variance and multiplied by the frozen `K` — 1.4633955775862155 for `pstar`, 1.488567942168242 for `pstar15` — calibrated so the five-year peak margin usage is 90%.
- Cadence: rebalance every 5 sessions anchored to 2021-11-18. Weights move only on those sessions; the decision after the 2026-11-19 close is taken and sealed as `reports/live/r14_targets_2026-11-19.json` (`rebalance_due` true), to be traded at the 2026-11-20 open, and the next one falls after the 2026-11-27 close, inside the final-exit schedule, which therefore adds no exposure.
- Caps: **any** ticker at ≤38% of initial assets, with SPY overflow routed to DIA and then QQQ by their 60-day beta to SPY clipped to [0.5, 1.5]; `0.5 × long gross + 0.6 × short gross ≤ 90%` of equity (R20); ≤100 positions and ≤77 shorts (R6). Discretionary holdings are counted against margin and position count and are **never traded** by the strategy: a position with no matching strategy order in the journal is flagged in Slack as discretionary or unclassified, not sold.
- Eligibility: a sleeve ETF must price ≥$1 with fund size ≥$500M or it is **skipped**, its weight is not redistributed, and the deviation from the published book is reported in Slack (R2). A short requires affirmative current shortable + easy-to-borrow evidence and ≥$1B market cap; an un-borrowable name is **skipped, never replaced** (R5). Unknown borrow is a veto, not a default.
- Execution: whole shares at the next open; a name rounding below one share is skipped. On a rebalance session an existing position is re-sized only outside the 0.25%-of-equity band, while entries and exits always trade. Between rebalances **nothing may add exposure** (R19) except the 39%→38% trim (R8) and the margin cut above 95%→90%. −8% from the persistent peak flattens and latches a halt until manual recovery (R9). P\* configures **no daily-loss entry halt** (`daily_loss_limit` is null), so a bad day does not by itself stop entries — the between-rebalance no-add rule and the drawdown latch are what limit it, and a plan must not claim a −2% stop that the configuration does not have. Limit orders only, DAY, regular trading hours, ≤25 bps of a fresh quote, quote and account age ≤60 s, approval TTL ≤300 s. Kill/recovery state survives process and Session restart, and recovery cannot waive a hard rule or unlock a stale release.
- Orders: the go-live day is **one ENTRY batch** — a full book names 82 targets, and the batch shrinks with the prices because a name rounding below one whole share is skipped without redistribution. Measured at the recorded 2026-11-19 closes that is 75 orders on `pstar15` (5 sleeve longs + 70 of 77 shorts) and 70 on `pstar` (65 shorts), and the sealed 2026-11-19 rebalance book also gives 75, against an 87-position five-year maximum. Ordinary trading executes at most **100 strategy orders per ET day** (reductions are exempt); the other 100 of the competition's 200 stay reserved for an emergency flatten, and the planner refuses to consume that reserve. Partial fill events are not separate executed orders.

Role outputs are separate immutable envelopes under the desired paths in `templates.json`. They are future runtime contracts; these directories are not provisioned here. Relay alone writes durable append-only `journal/decisions.jsonl` and `journal/fills.jsonl`; roles submit structured events, never edit the journal or each other's artifacts. Use unique event IDs and atomic deduplication, UTC timestamps, schema validation, account fencing and flush/fsync before acknowledging durable acceptance. A changed payload under an existing event ID is a conflict, not an update.

Decision fields: `event_id, timestamp, mode, account_id, run_id, session_id, agent, kind, symbol, strategy_id, intent, action, reason, rule_checks, target_checksum, order_fingerprint, config_checksum`. `strategy_id` is what makes a journal row attributable to a P\* variant: `live/pstar.py` treats a position with no journaled ALLOW/SUBMITTED/FILLED row for the configured `strategy_id` as discretionary, and `rules/gate_pstar.py` refuses any order in it, so the field must be present on every order decision.

Fill fields: `event_id, timestamp, mode, account_id, run_id, session_id, order_id, fill_id, symbol, side, quantity, price, commission, platform_fee, regulatory_fee, slippage_bps, arrival_price`.
