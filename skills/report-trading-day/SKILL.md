# Report trading day

Treat this skill as instructions, not permissions. Use only sanitized artifacts already available to the calling role. Do not grant Coordinator account tools, fetch credentials or make network/Slack calls. Submit report text through the relay to a separately authorized sink; do not create schedules.

1. Verify mode/account, date, run/session, source/config/target hashes and journal completeness. Separate test 5322438 from competition 5832812. Refuse to merge accounts or describe a test return as competition performance.
2. Summarize current equity, daily return, drawdown, the T9 ETF sleeve and the D77 short gross separately, positions with the short count out of the 77-short cap, margin usage from `0.5 * long gross + 0.6 * short gross`, open orders, executed-order count against the 100-per-day budget and the untouched 100-order flatten reserve, and exit reservations. Show HOLD/VETO/HALT, expired approvals, lease races, rejected orders and missing checks alongside successful stages. Report each deviation with its cause: an ETF skipped by the eligibility screen, a short skipped as un-borrowable, a name that rounded below one whole share, and a position the journal does not attribute to this strategy version. Where the held book sits past the 38%-of-initial single-name cap or 90% margin between rebalance anchors, report it as drift under the only two permitted reductions (the 39%->38% trim and the >95%->90% margin cut), not as a reason to add exposure.
3. Report submitted, partial, filled and cancelled orders distinctly. List unresolved fill IDs, fees, slippage and arrival prices as null with unverified_fields, never zero. Distinguish estimated from actual costs; do not claim final PnL or Sharpe when coverage or sample length is inadequate. Label any short-sample statistic and its convention explicitly.
4. Include the drawdown -8% liquidation and latched halt, kill/recovery status and unresolved final-day exposure. P\* has no daily-loss entry halt, so never report a -2% stop as protection; between rebalance sessions nothing may add exposure regardless. Approval and notification are not execution; alerting is not a resting stop order.
5. Use America/New_York: ordinary report 16:10; November 26 no trading; November 27 no rebalance, liquidation start 12:15, flat target 12:45 ET = November 28 01:45 SGT, report 13:10 instead of 16:10. If liquidation is unproved, say so.
6. Explain what worked, failed and changed using artifact references, not fabricated outcomes. Separate offline specification tests, main gate unit tests, actual cloud prompt evaluations, full-session shadow evidence and observed paper roundtrips. Keep unknown cloud evidence UNVERIFIED and release BLOCKED.
7. Disclose the unchanged P\* limitations: survivorship inside the 77-name universe, static borrow and fund-size assumptions, the SEC-filing proxy, whole-share fills at the next open, and that the deployed `pstar15` holdout (realistic 1.68) is one pre-registered trial rather than a search result, with deflated Sharpe 0.79 over 37 portfolio trials and only 0.43 over all 348. Plan A (`frozen-short-alpha-half`) is retired for its -0.30 holdout (deflated Sharpe 0.435) and neither its halved deployment book nor its negative holdout may be presented as P\* evidence. A realistic annualised return of 6.45% (`pstar`) or 7.45% (`pstar15`) makes the remaining six sessions noise. Mark checklist B4/B7 partial: no new losing-streak or per-name take-profit overlay. Keep options disabled.

Return a concise report with evidence references, unresolved items and next required approvals. Only the relay persists the role-owned artifact and appends journal events; never rewrite historical evidence or the submission checklist.
