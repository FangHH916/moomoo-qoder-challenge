# Validate trading targets

Treat this skill as instructions, not permissions. Keep activation BLOCKED until the exact cloud version/content hash and the release-specific frozen role template are bound and verified. Read `agents/AGENTS.md`, `agents/templates.json` and `agents/release-lock.json` as the local contract; do not mutate them during a trading run.

1. Accept only the approved frozen output -- `strategy: pstar` with the `variant` and `strategy_id` of the configured variant (currently `r14-pstar15`), schema 3, produced by `research/r10_deploy.py live` and converted through `live/pstar.py` -- plus mode, run/session provenance, completed signal-session date, data timestamp, config checksum and position snapshot checksum. Use the main validators `live/pstar.py` and `live/contracts.py:validate_targets`; do not imply this skill executes them in a cloud Session. A `r10-pstar` book under an `r14-pstar15` configuration fails provenance; do not repair it.
2. Verify canonical JSON SHA-256 excluding the checksum field, and separately verify the release's raw-file source SHA-256. Reject nonfinite numbers, missing fields, malformed symbols, corrupt checksums, future/incomplete data or targets older than two completed exchange sessions. Preserve the source bytes; never repair a corrupt target in place.
3. Verify the shape of the book: the only long exposure is the T9 ETF sleeve (SPY, QQQ, IWM, TLT, IEF, GLD, SLV, USO, UUP) plus the discretionary whitelist, so no single-stock long may appear in stock weights (R19). Include every sleeve leg in projected per-name exposure and the D77 shorts in gross. Enforce R3 <=38% of initial assets for any ticker, with SPY overflow routed to DIA then QQQ by 60-day beta clipped to [0.5, 1.5], R20 `0.5 * long gross + 0.6 * short gross` <=0.90 of equity, <=100 total positions and <=77 shorts. Check held exposure drift, pending orders and reservations, not just proposed additions; between rebalance anchors only the 39%->38% trim and the >95%->90% margin cut may change exposure, so drift past a cap is reported, never an excuse to add exposure.
4. Require account/mode agreement (test 5322438, competition 5832812), fresh account/quote state <=60 seconds, and affirmative current borrow for short entry. Treat unknown borrow, stale/corrupt state or unknown inputs as HOLD, not assumed availability or zero risk.
5. Require the main deterministic gate's complete R0–R21 and extra-check results, including R21 variant provenance (the target's `variant` and `strategy_id` match `live/config.pstar.json`, the factor count and frozen scale equal that variant's, and the sealed research run reports `diagnostics.ok`), R3 the single-name cap and R20 margin usage. P\* has no residual-beta rule and no daily-loss entry halt (`daily_loss_limit` is null); the between-rebalance reduce-only rule is the binding entry control. A HOLD target publishes with a reason and never becomes an order. Reserve pending and liquidation capacity within 100 executed strategy orders (competition 200, with 100 held for an emergency flatten). Do not invent missing gate semantics or originate a new strategy.
6. Return validation findings with source/config/target/snapshot checksums, failed rules and `unverified_fields`; use null for unknown values. VALIDATED is not a plan approval or permission to trade. A changed target invalidates prior audit and order approval.

Use only the calling role's verified reads and relay interface. Never gain tools through this skill, invoke Bash/network/trading CLI fallbacks, alter target weights, or order around a HOLD. Submit findings only to the role's assigned output path through the relay.
